Recipe 12 of 100 · Mexico · Americas

Elote (Mexican Street Corn)

  • 20 min
  • Serves 4
  • Heat: Warm
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat a grill or cast-iron pan to medium-high. Brush the corn with the oil.
  2. Cook the corn 10-12 min, turning every 2-3 min, until kernels are tender and charred in spots.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, sour cream, garlic, salt and the juice of half a lime.
  4. Brush the warm corn generously with the sauce while the heat is still on the kernels so it clings.
  5. Roll each ear in the cotija so it coats the surface, then dust with chili powder and cilantro.
  6. Serve with the remaining lime cut in wedges to squeeze over.

Pairs with Mexican lager with lime

Chef’s move Cut kernels off and toss with the sauce for esquites, a spoonable version for parties.

Recipe 21 of 100 · Italy · Europe

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

  • 20 min
  • Serves 4
  • Heat: Warm
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil 4 quarts of water with 1 tbsp salt. Cook the spaghetti 1 min short of the package time, then scoop out 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil and garlic in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Cook 3-4 min, stirring, until the garlic is pale gold. Do not let it brown.
  3. Add the pepper flakes and 1/2 cup of pasta water to the skillet. It will sputter, then simmer for 1 min.
  4. Add the drained spaghetti and the 1/2 tsp salt. Toss over medium heat for 1-2 min, adding splashes of water, until the oil and water form a glossy sauce clinging to the strands.
  5. Off the heat, toss in the parsley. Serve right away, with cheese if you like.

Pairs with Crisp arugula salad with lemon

Chef’s move Start the garlic in cold oil so it infuses gently; sliced garlic that hits hot oil turns bitter in seconds.

Recipe 26 of 100 · Kenya · Africa

Sukuma Wiki with Fried Eggs

  • 25 min
  • Serves 4
  • Heat: Warm
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Method

  1. Strip the stems from the greens, stack the leaves, roll them up and slice into thin ribbons. Wash and drain well.
  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 5 min until soft and golden, then add garlic, chili, coriander and turmeric and stir 30 sec.
  3. Add the tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt. Cook 5 min, mashing, until they break down into a thick sauce.
  4. Add the greens in handfuls, wilting each before the next. Add the water and remaining salt, cover and cook 8 min until tender but still green.
  5. Fry the eggs in 1 tbsp oil over medium heat until the whites are firm. Serve the greens topped with eggs and lemon wedges.

Pairs with Ugali or warm chapati

Chef’s move Slice the greens as thin as you can; fine ribbons cook fast and stay silky rather than stringy.
